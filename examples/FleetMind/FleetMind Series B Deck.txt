Slide 2 — The Problem

Last-mile delivery is the most expensive segment of the logistics chain, representing 41–53% of total delivery cost (McKinsey, 2023). A 100-vehicle delivery fleet operating in a metro market generates approximately $2.3M in annual operating cost from three controllable sources: fuel/energy (28%), labor (54%), and vehicle downtime (18%). Route optimization, driver performance management, and predictive maintenance have existed as software categories for 15 years, but adoption remains fragmented and the tools don't talk to each other.

The result: the average commercial fleet operates at 67% route efficiency (actual miles driven vs. optimal route), experiences 2.3 vehicle breakdowns per 100 vehicles per month, and pays $14,000–$22,000 per driver per year in avoidable overtime.

Why has this been hard to solve? Three reasons:
1. Data silos: telematics vendors, ERP systems, HR platforms, and maintenance systems don't integrate.
2. Driver resistance: previous "surveillance" tools created labor relations problems that caused operators to disable features.
3. Complexity: optimizing across routes, driver schedules, vehicle health, and customer time windows simultaneously requires compute and algorithms that were not viable at a reasonable price until 2021–2022.

FleetMind solves all three.

Slide 4 — The Product

FleetMind is an AI-native fleet operations platform that integrates all data streams (telematics via OBD-II, ERP via API, HR/scheduling via API, maintenance records) and provides real-time optimization across four domains:

1. Dynamic Route Optimization: AI-driven routing that updates in real-time based on traffic, weather, customer availability windows, and vehicle capacity. Reduces average miles driven per stop by 18% vs. static routing.

2. Driver Performance & Coaching: Continuous monitoring of driving behavior (speed, braking, acceleration, idle time) with in-cab coaching alerts. Critically, FleetMind's coaching is privacy-by-design: event data is not shared with dispatchers unless the driver consents or safety thresholds are exceeded. This design decision has been critical to union acceptance.

3. Predictive Maintenance: ML models trained on 3.2M vehicle-months of telematics data predict component failure 8–14 days in advance with 79% accuracy for high-criticality components (brakes, transmission, cooling system). Average unplanned downtime reduction: 61%.

4. Fleet Intelligence Dashboard: Operational visibility across the fleet with anomaly detection, automated reporting, and integration with insurance carrier data for premium optimization.

FleetMind is hardware-agnostic (supports any OBD-II device) and integrates with SAP, Oracle TMS, McLeod, and 14 other fleet management systems via standard APIs.

Slide 12 — Competitive Landscape

Samsara (NASDAQ: IOT): Market leader in fleet telematics, $2.4B ARR. Hardware-driven model (Samsara camera systems). Not as strong on AI optimization or predictive maintenance. Losing enterprise deals where AI fleet intelligence is the priority. We see Samsara in 45% of competitive deals; win rate 38% against them.

Motive (formerly KeepTruckin): $1B+ ARR, trucking-focused. Strong on ELD compliance (regulatory). Weaker on optimization and predictive analytics. Win rate: 61% against Motive.

Geotab: Strong in Europe and Canada. Less competitive in U.S. enterprise market. Win rate: 70% against Geotab.

Platform Science: Telematics OS for trucking, strong integration marketplace. Partner, not direct competitor in most cases.

Lytx: Video-based driver safety. Strong in insurance use cases. Not competing on optimization. Complementary.
