GRIDLY CUSTOMER CASE STUDY
Customer: Brookfield Property Partners
Sites: 22 office and retail properties across California and New York
Deployment period: Pilot (3 sites) March 2023; full rollout through Q3 2024
Prepared: December 2024

---

CUSTOMER BACKGROUND

Brookfield Property Partners manages approximately 130 million square feet of commercial real estate globally. The North America commercial portfolio (office + retail) is approximately 68 million square feet. Brookfield's U.S. portfolio faces significant electricity cost pressure in California and New York, two of the highest demand-charge states in the country.

Prior to Gridly, Brookfield's energy management strategy was a combination of LED retrofits, HVAC scheduling optimization, and utility demand response enrollment through Enel X. Average electricity cost across the Gridly-enrolled portfolio: $1.47/sq ft/year. Average demand charges as a percentage of electricity bill: 48%.

---

PILOT RESULTS (3 SITES, Q2–Q3 2023)

Site 1: 210,000 sq ft Class A office, Century City, CA
- Pre-Gridly monthly demand charge: $31,400
- Post-Gridly monthly demand charge: $0 (fully eliminated)
- Gridly monthly service fee: $24,800
- Net monthly savings: $6,600
- Annualized savings: $79,200

Site 2: 185,000 sq ft mixed-use retail, San Francisco, CA
- Pre-Gridly monthly demand charge: $24,100
- Post-Gridly monthly demand charge: $1,200 (partial reduction due to irregular HVAC load profile)
- Gridly monthly service fee: $18,400
- Net monthly savings: $4,500
- Annualized savings: $54,000

Site 3: 320,000 sq ft office tower, Midtown Manhattan, NY
- Pre-Gridly monthly demand charge: $44,600
- Post-Gridly monthly demand charge: $800
- Gridly monthly service fee: $34,700
- Net monthly savings: $9,100
- Annualized savings: $109,200

Pilot aggregate results:
Total annualized savings to Brookfield: $242,400
Average demand charge reduction: 94.3%
Average electricity cost reduction (total bill): 31.8%
Battery system uptime across 3 sites: 99.1%

---

EXPANSION DECISION

Following the pilot, Brookfield's U.S. head of asset management and CFO jointly approved expansion to 19 additional sites. Decision criteria cited in internal approval memo (shared with permission):
1. Positive cash flow from day one (no capex required)
2. Pilot performance exceeded underwritten savings by 14%
3. Demand response revenue (shared with Brookfield as contract kicker above guaranteed fee) added unexpected upside
4. ESG reporting benefit: Gridly deployment reduces scope 2 emissions for buildings by enabling greater solar charging

Expansion rollout timeline: Q4 2023 through Q3 2024. All 22 sites fully operational as of September 2024.
