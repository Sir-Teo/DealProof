Slide 2 — The Problem

Healthcare documentation is a $8.3B annual burden on the U.S. system. Physicians spend an average of 4.5 hours per day on paperwork — nearly as much time as they spend with patients. Clinical notes, prior authorizations, referral letters, discharge summaries, and insurance appeals are drafted manually, checked manually, and submitted manually. This is not a technology problem that has gone unaddressed — it is a workflow problem that existing tools have failed to solve.

EHR systems (Epic, Cerner, Oracle Health) have text-generation features but they produce generic, legally exposed drafts that physicians routinely rewrite. Medical scribes cost $30,000–$60,000 per physician per year and introduce transcription error. Voice dictation has been available for 20 years and has not eliminated documentation burden because the downstream structuring, coding, and submission steps remain manual.

NeuraForm automates the full documentation lifecycle — not just the dictation layer.

Slide 4 — The Product

NeuraForm is a clinical documentation platform that integrates directly with Epic and Cerner via certified FHIR APIs. It records the patient encounter (ambient listening mode), generates a structured clinical note, auto-populates the ICD-10 and CPT codes, pre-fills prior authorization forms, and routes for physician sign-off in a single workflow.

Core capabilities:
1. Ambient Clinical Documentation — real-time AI note generation during encounters, trained on 12M de-identified clinical notes
2. Prior Authorization Automation — 94% of payer forms completed without physician input; decision in <4 hours vs. industry average 3.1 days
3. Coding Accuracy Engine — 97.3% first-pass coding accuracy vs. 83% industry average, reducing claim denials
4. Referral Intelligence — auto-generates referral letters with relevant history, flags missing workup items before routing

NeuraForm is FDA-registered as a Class II medical device (510(k) clearance pending for diagnostic suggestion module). All processing is HIPAA-compliant. Data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure.

Slide 12 — Competitive Landscape

Nuance DAX / Microsoft: Voice dictation only. No PA automation, no coding integration. $15–20K/physician/year. Incumbent at many sites but losing share to ambient competitors. Epic partnership announced but product not integrated as of our analysis.

Abridge: Strong product, $150M Series C raised February 2025. Pure ambient documentation, no PA or coding. We see them in 20% of our competitive deals. They win on physician experience; we win on breadth of automation and ROI to the CFO.

Suki: Consumer-priced ($300/month) and positioning at small practices. Not a competitor in enterprise health systems.

Nabla: EU-based, limited U.S. presence, no FHIR integration with Epic.

Our differentiation: We are the only vendor with live prior authorization automation integrated into the clinical workflow. That is the highest-value pain point for CFOs and the feature that converts deals fastest.
