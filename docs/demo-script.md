# 2-3 Minute Demo Script

DealProof is a red team analyst for venture and private-equity diligence. Raylu helps investors find and understand companies; DealProof pressure-tests the claims before a partner takes the deal to IC.

Start with Harvey AI, a fictional legal AI company selling into AmLaw 100 firms and Fortune 500 in-house legal teams. I load the pitch deck, founder-call transcript, financial snapshot, market notes, and website capture.

DealProof extracts the claims that actually matter: growth rate, market size, ROI, retention, pricing, competition, compliance scope, and financial traction. This is not a deck summary. It is a claim ledger.

Now the app grades the claims. Some are strong: the financial snapshot supports committed ARR, ACV, gross margin, burn, and cash position. Some are weak: the market-size claim and customer productivity claims need independent validation. The OpenAI dependency claim is not a surprise buried in the memo; it becomes a gating diligence request because the current materials admit there is no multi-model redundancy.

The evidence drawer shows the investor exactly why each grade was assigned, with citations back to the uploaded materials or public-research placeholders.

Finally, DealProof exports a red team memo for IC: the investment question, strengths, material risks, diligence asks, and recommendation. The conclusion is not just "invest" or "pass." It is a sharper next step: proceed only if references validate ROI, market sizing, security posture, and model-dependency mitigation.

The technical point is simple: messy deal materials become structured, cited judgment. The commercial point is even simpler: every investor wants to avoid being fooled by a great story that breaks under diligence.
