# 2-3 Minute Demo Script

DealProof is a red team analyst for venture and private-equity diligence. Raylu helps investors find and understand companies; DealProof pressure-tests the claims before a partner takes the deal to IC.

Start with a fictional company, CaviClear AI, which says it is the fastest-growing AI billing platform for dental clinics. I load the pitch deck, founder-call transcript, financial snapshot, and website capture.

DealProof extracts the claims that actually matter: growth rate, market size, ROI, retention, pricing, competition, compliance scope, and financial traction. This is not a deck summary. It is a claim ledger.

Now the app grades the claims. Some are green: ARR grew from $82k to $235k and the compliance posture says human review is required. Some are weak: the ROI claim sounds compelling, but there is no customer-level methodology. One is contradicted: the company says there are no direct competitors, but the evidence review finds dental RCM vendors with denial-management workflows. One is missing: the $6B market size has no bottom-up proof.

The evidence drawer shows the investor exactly why each grade was assigned, with citations back to the uploaded materials or public-research placeholders.

Finally, DealProof exports a one-page red team memo for IC: the investment question, strengths, material risks, diligence asks, and recommendation. The conclusion is not "invest" or "pass." It is a sharper next step: proceed only if references validate ROI and the team replaces unsupported market and competition claims.

The technical point is simple: messy deal materials become structured, cited judgment. The commercial point is even simpler: every investor wants to avoid being fooled by a great story that breaks under diligence.
