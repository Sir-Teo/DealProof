# Devpost Description

DealProof is an AI diligence red team for VC and PE investors. A user uploads messy deal materials such as a pitch deck, founder-call transcript, company URL, financial snapshot, or data-room folder. DealProof extracts the important claims, verifies them against the uploaded packet and public-source evidence, assigns risk grades, generates follow-up diligence questions, and exports a partner-ready investment committee memo.

The main demo uses Harvey AI, a fictional legal AI company selling into AmLaw 100 firms and Fortune 500 in-house legal teams. DealProof turns the pitch deck, founder-call transcript, financial snapshot, market notes, and website capture into a structured claim ledger across ARR, gross margin, customer expansion, legal-workflow ROI, competition, security, regulatory exposure, and model dependency. A second SynthPay demo shows the same workflow for an AI payments company.

For example, Harvey's financial materials support committed ARR, ACV, burn, cash position, and current gross margin. But customer-productivity and legal-AI market claims need stronger independent validation. The claim that there are no meaningful competitors is weakened by incumbent legal-research, contract-lifecycle, and workflow vendors. The OpenAI dependency is not buried in a memo; it becomes a gating diligence request because the current materials admit there is no multi-model redundancy today.

The output is not just a summary. DealProof creates investor judgment: a dashboard of claim risk, an evidence drawer with citations, questions to ask before IC, and a concise red team memo that a partner could use immediately.

Raylu helps private-market investors find and understand companies. DealProof fits after sourcing: once a company enters the pipeline, it pressure-tests the founder narrative before the investment team trusts it.
