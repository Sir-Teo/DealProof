# Devpost Description

DealProof is an AI diligence red team for VC and PE investors. A user uploads messy deal materials such as a pitch deck, founder-call transcript, company URL, financial snapshot, or data-room folder. DealProof extracts the important claims, verifies them against the uploaded packet and public-source evidence, assigns risk grades, generates follow-up diligence questions, and exports a partner-ready investment committee memo.

The demo uses a fictional startup, CaviClear AI, which claims to be the fastest-growing AI billing platform for dental clinics. DealProof turns that story into a structured claim ledger across market size, ARR growth, customer ROI, competition, pricing, retention, compliance, and financials. It then separates supported claims from weak, contradicted, and missing-evidence claims.

For example, the uploaded financial snapshot supports ARR growth from $82k to $235k. But the claim that clinics save 18 hours per week is weak because there is no customer-level methodology. The claim that there are no direct competitors is contradicted by dental revenue-cycle vendors with denial-management workflows. The $6B market-size claim is missing bottom-up evidence.

The output is not just a summary. DealProof creates investor judgment: a dashboard of claim risk, an evidence drawer with citations, questions to ask before IC, and a concise red team memo that a partner could use immediately.

Raylu helps private-market investors find and understand companies. DealProof fits after sourcing: once a company enters the pipeline, it pressure-tests the founder narrative before the investment team trusts it.
